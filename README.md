# First Apartment Budget Comparator
**By Meredith Makowsky – Accounting Major, Sykes College of Business**  
**University of Tampa**

## Overview
This interactive Excel tool helps compare apartment budgets realistically. Users input details like rent, utilities, groceries, transportation, and other expenses, and the tool automatically calculates total monthly and annual costs, compares scenarios, and visualizes the results with charts. It’s designed to promote financial awareness and smart decision-making for young adults moving into their first apartment.

## Key Features
- **User-friendly Inputs & Dropdowns** — Easy data entry with predefined categories and dropdown selections for common expenses.
- **Automatic Calculations** — Real-time totals, percentage breakdowns, and affordability checks (e.g., rent-to-income ratio).
- **Scenario Comparisons** — Side-by-side views of different apartment options or lifestyle choices.
- **Dynamic Charts** — Built-in Excel charts showing expense distribution, monthly cash flow, and budget vs. actual comparisons.
- **AI-assisted Development** — Leveraged AI for advanced formula design, error checking, and optimization suggestions.

## How AI Was Used in This Project
Artificial intelligence played a collaborative role in building this tool, accelerating development and enhancing its functionality. As an accounting student, I used AI as a "co-pilot" to handle complex Excel tasks while maintaining full control over the final logic and accuracy.

- **Formula Design & Automation** — I prompted AI tools (such as ChatGPT, Grok, or Excel's built-in Copilot features) to generate sophisticated formulas for conditional calculations, dynamic ranges, and nested IF/VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP combinations. This was especially useful for creating automatic scenario switches and affordability alerts.
  
- **Debugging & Optimization** — When formulas produced errors or were inefficient, I used AI for explanations and suggested fixes, optimizing for performance and best practices.

- **Data Validation & Insights** — AI assisted in designing data validation rules, conditional formatting, and realistic default values based on national averages.

All AI-generated content was carefully reviewed, tested, and customized by me to ensure accuracy and alignment with accounting principles. AI acted as an accelerator, letting me focus on the financial logic and user experience.

## How to Use
1. Download and open the `.xlsx` file in Microsoft Excel (desktop version recommended).
2. Go to the **Inputs** sheet and enter your details (income, rent options, estimated utilities, etc.).
3. Watch the **Summary** and **Charts** sheets update automatically.
4. Adjust inputs to compare different scenarios—perfect for lease negotiations or lifestyle planning!
